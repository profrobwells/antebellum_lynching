The Picayune gives a most dreadful account of the
application of the lynch law in the West. It states that a
company of armed men in Phillips County, (Ark) set out in
pursuit of a set of counterfeiters and horse thieves, whom
they overtook 40 miles below Helena, on the Mississippi
River. They were taken and secured, and the whole number,
twenty-seven, were tied hand and foot and drowned in the
Mississippi at Island 69, in the presence of two men;
Harrod and Burges, who, it appears, officiated or at least
took an active part in the execution of the sentence. This
dreadful account is confirmed by the New Orleans Picayune
of yesterday.
