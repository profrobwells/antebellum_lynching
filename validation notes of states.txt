California homicide introduced by the Chronicle 122 persons
had suffered violent deaths at the hands of their fellow
men in this State in the first four months of the year,
including fourteen hung by Lynch law and two hung according
to statute law."

The Mobile Daily Commercial register anbd Patriot reported on August 18, 1841 that 27 men were lynched by drowning in the Mississippi River: 
"It states that a
company of armed men in Phillips County, (Ark) set out in

From the South Branch Intelligencer on Sept. 5, 1835: " R. W. Carroll, a hairdresser of Charleston, S.C., was lynched on the 21st ult.
The mob seized him between 8 and 9 o'clock in the morning, gave him 20 lashes,
a coat of tar and cotton, from head to foot, then marched through several
principal streets and lodged him in jail. The account states that respectable
citizens lent their presence, and all was "orderly conducted!" His offense was
dealing with, and receiving stolen goods from slaves, against which he had
repeatedly been warned. "
